 
## 📅 Monday Launcher

App Mockup. A weekly planning tool that captures unfinished tasks on Friday evening and delivers a clean, organized brief Monday morning, so you start the week already knowing who to call, why, and what to say.

### The problem it solves
You spend all week stressed about things you need to follow up on. Friday comes, you finally decompress. By Monday morning you've forgotten everything and you're already behind before the day starts.

### How it works
1. Friday at 5 PM: a notification prompts you to log pre-rescheduled emails and Monday morning, planned phone calls
2. You fill in quick details: who, why, what to say, phone number, address
3. Monday morning: a clean digest hands it all back to you, organized and ready

### Features (prototype)
- Friday wrap-up screen with expandable email and call entries
- Optional fields for fax numbers, office hours, and addresses
- Monday morning digest with tap-to-expand call cards
- Available in dark mode and paper/notebook aesthetic
